03 — Research

Clinically grounded medical AI

Doctoral research at Tsinghua's Laboratory of Minimally Invasive Diagnosis and Therapy and 3D Imaging, now carried into product engineering at Ant Group.

Medical image intelligence

Segmentation, deformable registration, multimodal fusion and ultrasound enhancement, with robust learning-based methods for clinically meaningful image analysis.

Multimodal AI for diagnosis and treatment

Frameworks that integrate CT, MRI, gene mutation data and electronic health records to support diagnosis, treatment optimization and prognostic assessment.

Interventional and surgical computing

Intelligent minimally invasive diagnosis and therapy, robotic perception and image-guided intervention, connecting computational models with procedures.

Research translation

Bridging academic medical AI and engineering deployment, from university-based innovation to product-oriented medical AI at Ant Group.

04 — About

Experience and education

Khor Hee Guan received his B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from Chongqing University in 2019, then his M.Eng. (2022) and Ph.D. (2026) in Biomedical Engineering from Tsinghua University, where he was named a Xinghua Scholar.
